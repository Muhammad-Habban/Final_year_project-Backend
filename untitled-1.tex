\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Explanation}
\begin{itemize}
\item The text discusses several tools used for measuring different things. 
\item A measuring cylinder, which is often made of glass or transparent plastic, is used to measure the volume of a liquid or powdered substance. 
\item It can also be used to measure the volume of an irregularly shaped solid object by using a method called the displacement method.
\item A screw gauge, or micrometer screw gauge, is a tool used to measure small lengths with a lot of accuracy. 
\item It consists of a U-shaped frame with a hollow cylinder (or sleeve) that has a millimeter scale over it. 
\item Inside a part called the Thimble, there is a threaded spindle that moves along the index line each time the thimble completes a rotation.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Explanation (cont.)}
\begin{itemize}
\item Beam balances are a type of scale used to measure mass. 
\item An unknown mass is placed in one pan and is balanced by placing known masses in the other pan.
\item Measuring tapes, which can be made of cotton, metal, or plastic, are used to measure length in meters and centimeters.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Example}
\begin{itemize}
\item For example, if you were baking a cake and needed to measure out 200ml of milk, you could use a measuring cylinder to do this. 
\item Or, if you are a jeweler and need to measure the thickness of a small piece of metal with high accuracy, you might use a screw gauge.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\end{itemize}
